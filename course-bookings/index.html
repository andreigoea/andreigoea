How the numbers are worked out

Revenue is the Total (£) of confirmed bookings only. Six rows that repeated an earlier row exactly were removed first. Cancelled bookings are counted by their booking date. Half-years are Oct–Mar and Apr–Sep.

Tidying done before the data was attached: course names that differed only by capitals or spaces were merged, "PM Essentials" was read as Project Management Essentials, two dates written as day/month/year were converted, and four blank totals were filled as delegates × price per delegate.
